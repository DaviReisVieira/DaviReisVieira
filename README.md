# 🧭 **About Me**

> "Be a pilot of your dreams. Own your head. Never let others put words where there are no ideas — or ideas where you never wanted a word."

I'm **Davi Reis**, part of the founding team at [Refer](https://liarefer.com).

I started coding young and went through hacking, product, automation, AI and infrastructure. These days most of my time goes into one problem: careers are still navigated by hand.

At Refer, we're flipping the entire recruiting model upside down: talent doesn't "apply" — they're **represented**. A career agent, **Lia**, learns what people are looking for and helps them reach the right companies and decision makers.
